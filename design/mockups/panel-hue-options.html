Trade-offs

OptionWinsRisks
A · Soft side tintKeeps your hue idea, fixes the washed-out cards/text, weakest visual risk.Still two surface hues to maintain.
B · Hue in mainConversation is the hero; sides recede into neutral chrome.Tinted reading surface can fatigue over long sessions; code/diff contrast needs re-checking.
C · Neutral + linesZero tint maintenance; calmest, most IDE-like.Relies on divider weight; can feel flat if lines are too faint.
D · CurrentBaseline for comparison.Strongest tint with untuned side content — the thing that looks off.
